---
name: StudyXP product direction
description: Durable product decisions for the StudyXP student companion experience.
---

StudyXP should feel like a living growth companion rather than a school administration tool. Sprig, meaningful progress, and the idea of returning every day are core emotional anchors.

**Why:** The product brief explicitly prioritizes an emotional experience built around growth beyond grades.

**How to apply:** Preserve the dark navy and emerald/lime direction, rewarding but non-gamified XP, and Sprig's concise encouragement when extending the product.
