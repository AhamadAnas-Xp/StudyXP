You are a senior UI/UX designer, startup founder, React developer, and AI engineer. Build a complete modern mobile-first application called "StudyXP".

MISSION
StudyXP is an AI-powered study coach that helps students grow academically through gamification, AI coaching, personalized learning, and progress tracking.

Brand Message:
"Grow Beyond Grades."

The app should NOT feel like a school app.
It should feel like a premium Apple-quality startup.

• Rounded corners

• Smooth animations

• Premium startup feel
• Consistent spacing
• Friendly typography
